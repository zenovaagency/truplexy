# Truplexy — Free Trial and Cancelling

## How does the free trial work?
Starter and Pro start with a 7-day free trial. You add a payment method to start it, and nothing is charged during the trial. When the trial ends, your payment method is charged for the plan and billing period you chose, and the subscription renews automatically until you cancel.

## Do I need a card for the trial?
Yes. A payment method is required to start the 7-day trial. You are not charged during the trial.

## How do I avoid being charged after the trial?
Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing. Cancel in the dashboard under Settings → Plan & usage, or in the billing portal.

## Can I try Truplexy more than once?
The free trial is limited to one per customer.

## Does Enterprise have a trial?
No. Enterprise is set up with the sales team. Use https://truplexy.com/demo?topic=sales.

## How do I start a trial?
Pick a plan on https://truplexy.com/pricing, or create an account at https://app.truplexy.com. After sign-up and onboarding, the dashboard opens the plan's checkout.

## Can I cancel at any time?
Yes. Cancel a paid plan at any time in the dashboard or by emailing hello@truplexy.com. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep the plan and its limits until the end of the period you already paid for.

## What happens to my account when a plan ends?
Your account and data stay, but your bots stop replying until a new plan starts. Conversation history is kept for the shortest history period, which is 7 days while a business has no plan.

## Can I stop using Truplexy and delete everything?
Yes. You can stop using the service or ask for your business to be deleted at any time. A deleted business can be restored for 30 days. After that, its records, knowledge-base files and search vectors are permanently erased.
