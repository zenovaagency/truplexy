# Truplexy — Refund Policy

Full policy: https://truplexy.com/refunds

## What is the refund policy?
The first payment for a paid plan (Starter or Pro) is refundable in full within 14 days of purchase. After that, payments are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the period you paid for ends.

## When do the 14 days start?
They start on the day the payment is taken. If you started with the free trial, that is the day the trial ends and your payment method is first charged.

## Is the refund available more than once?
No. The 14-day refund applies once per customer. If you cancel, get a refund and later buy again, the new purchase is not eligible.

## What happens to my account after a refund?
A refund of a first payment ends the paid plan. Your account and its data stay, but your bots stop replying until a new plan starts.

## What is not refundable?
These are not refundable:
- Renewals of monthly or yearly plans. Cancel before the renewal date if you don't want to be charged.
- Token add-ons that have been used, and replies already billed against your balance, because the AI model cost has been incurred.
- Payments after the 14 days of your first payment, and upgrades after that window.
- Accounts closed for breaking the acceptable-use rules.
- Third-party costs, such as fees charged by messaging platforms or other services you connect.

## Can I get a refund for account balance I added?
If you ask within 14 days of adding funds, Truplexy refunds the part of that payment you have not used. Used funds and add-on tokens already consumed are not refundable. Add-on tokens expire at the end of the month they are bought in.

## What if I was charged the wrong amount?
If you were charged the wrong amount, charged twice, or charged after a cancellation that went through in time, tell Truplexy and it will fix it and refund the difference, whenever it is spotted. Report it as soon as you can.

## How do I ask for a refund?
Email hello@truplexy.com from the address on your account, or contact Paddle through the link in your payment receipt, within the 14 days. Give the business name and, if you have it, the order or invoice number.

## How long does a refund take?
Truplexy aims to reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds are issued through Paddle to your original payment method. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it. Refunds cannot go to a different payment method.

## Does the policy affect my legal rights?
No. It does not take away rights you have under the law where you live, such as a statutory cooling-off period. Those apply in addition to this policy.
