# Truplexy — Inbox, Human Handoff and Team

## What happens when a customer needs a person?
The assistant hands the conversation to your team with the full history and context, in the same unified inbox. A teammate takes over in one click, and the customer keeps talking on the channel they started on.

## What does the customer see when a person joins?
A notice such as "Name has joined and will reply here." When the person hands the chat back, the customer sees that too.

## Does the assistant stop when a ticket is escalated?
Yes. Escalating pauses the assistant on that conversation. A teammate must take over before replying.

## What do the ticket tabs and badges mean?
Tickets are open or closed. The inbox has a Needs reply tab and an Escalated tab, and an escalated ticket carries a single Escalated badge. A closed ticket that was escalated keeps a mark showing it.

## Do tickets close by themselves?
A ticket with no activity can auto-close after a set number of hours, from 1 to 2,160 hours. Setting 0 means never. A Keep open option prevents auto-close on a ticket.

## Can the assistant summarise a ticket?
Yes. On an escalated ticket, Generate summary writes a short summary of the conversation.

## Can the assistant draft a reply for my team?
Yes. Suggest reply in the ticket composer drafts an answer, with an optional hint and a Regenerate button. Nothing is sent until the agent presses Send. Each draft counts as one AI reply.

## Can tickets be assigned automatically?
Yes. Team members can have support roles (general, billing, technical, orders, returns, account, sales, product) and an availability (available, away, offline). Auto-assign has three modes: by category (the default), overall, or off. It rotates round-robin among available members who have had the dashboard open in the last 15 minutes.

## What team roles are there?
Owner, admin, editor, agent and viewer. Invitations last 7 days. Team size limits are 10 on Starter, 25 on Pro and 500 on Enterprise.

## Does it have analytics?
Yes. Conversation analytics and an Overview page are on every plan. The Overview includes AI replies, AI reply time, and how many conversations the AI resolved.

## Can I export tickets?
Yes. Tickets can be exported from the dashboard as a CSV or a zip.

## Does the assistant reply to hello and thanks instantly?
Yes. An English greeting or thanks gets an instant reply that does not count against your reply allowance.
