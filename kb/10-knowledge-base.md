# Truplexy — Knowledge Base

## How does the assistant know what to answer?
The assistant answers from the knowledge you give it: articles you write and files you upload. It can show customers the sources an answer came from. When it can't find a reliable answer it says so, asks a clarifying question, or offers a person instead of guessing.

## How do I add knowledge?
In the dashboard, open Knowledge. You can write an article with a title and Markdown content, or upload files. Short sections with clear headings retrieve best, because headings split an article into passages.

## Which file types can I upload?
You can upload Markdown, text, CSV, JSON, HTML, PDF and Word (.docx) files. The extensions are .md, .markdown, .txt, .csv, .json, .html, .htm, .pdf and .docx. Text files can be up to 5 MB, and HTML, PDF and Word files up to 25 MB.

## Can I upload an FAQ spreadsheet?
Yes. A CSV or JSON file becomes one section per row. The title column can be named title, question, name, topic or subject. The content column can be named content, answer, body, text or description.

## How big can an article be?
An article title can be up to 160 characters and the content up to 50,000 characters of Markdown. You can add an optional source link, which is shown with answers.

## How many documents can a bot have?
Starter allows 300 documents per bot. Pro allows 1,000. Enterprise allows 1,000, with custom limits available. A business with no plan can add up to 50.

## How soon can the assistant use a new article?
Right away for keyword matches, and by meaning after a minute or two.

## Can I import a website by URL?
No. There is no URL import. Add articles or upload files.

## What language should the knowledge be in?
English retrieves best. Customers can ask in other languages, but the closest passages are matched with lower confidence.

## Can the assistant learn from closed tickets?
Yes. Learn from closed tickets turns resolved conversations into suggested question-and-answer drafts on the Suggested tab. Your team reviews and approves each one, and Train bot adds approved drafts to the knowledge base. Each draft counts as one AI reply.

## Can I test what the assistant finds?
Yes. The Knowledge page has a retrieval test that shows which passages a question matches, without generating an answer.

## What if I upload the same content twice?
Truplexy detects identical content and doesn't add a duplicate. Uploading a file with the same name as an existing one replaces it.

## Does the assistant make things up?
It is told to answer in the passages' own words and never invent prices, features or links. It can still be wrong, so your team can read and correct any conversation.
