# Truplexy — WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram

These three channels connect from the dashboard under Integrations → Add a channel, through your own Meta app. A step-by-step guide in the dashboard walks you through it. Meta sets the rules for the messaging platforms, not Truplexy.

## How do I connect WhatsApp?
Truplexy answers customers on your WhatsApp Business number through your own Meta app. In the dashboard, open Integrations, choose WhatsApp and follow the guide. You will need these details from Meta: the Phone number ID, the WhatsApp Business Account ID, a system-user access token and the app secret.

## Do I need the WhatsApp Business app?
No. Meta walks you through adding a business phone number to the WhatsApp Business Platform, or picking one you already have.

## How do I connect Facebook Messenger?
Truplexy replies to messages sent to your Facebook Page through your own Meta app. You will need your Page ID, a Page access token and the app secret. Open Integrations in the dashboard, choose Messenger and follow the guide. In Meta's app settings, add the messaging subscriptions the guide lists.

## How do I connect Instagram?
Truplexy answers Instagram Direct messages to your professional account through your own Meta app and the Facebook Page it is linked to. Open Integrations in the dashboard, choose Instagram and follow the guide.

## Does it work with a personal Instagram account?
No. Instagram only offers messaging to professional accounts connected to a Facebook Page.

## Is setup one click?
No. Because it goes through your own Meta app, it takes setup on Meta's side and some time. Plan a good part of a day the first time, especially for WhatsApp Business verification.

## Is there a time limit for replying to a customer?
Yes. A team member cannot write to a customer more than 24 hours after the customer's last message on these Meta channels. Truplexy does not send template messages. The assistant replies within the window.

## Do these channels cost extra?
Truplexy includes WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram on Starter and Pro. Meta or other platforms may charge their own fees, and Truplexy does not refund third-party fees.

## What can the assistant do on these channels?
It answers from your knowledge base, hands the conversation to your team when needed, and every conversation lands in the same Truplexy inbox. The team's replies go back to the customer on the channel they started on.
