# Truplexy — Plans and Pricing

Prices are in US dollars and exclude any sales tax or VAT. Full comparison: https://truplexy.com/pricing

## How much does Truplexy cost?
Truplexy has three plans: Starter at $29 a month, Pro at $79 a month, and Enterprise at a price based on your volume. Starter and Pro both start with a 7-day free trial.

## How much is yearly billing?
Billed yearly, Starter is $23 a month ($276 a year) and Pro is $63 a month ($756 a year). Yearly billing saves 20% compared with paying monthly.

## What does the Starter plan include?
Starter costs $29 a month ($23 a month billed yearly). It includes:
- About 10,000 estimated AI replies and 10M AI tokens per month.
- 3 bots, 300 knowledge documents per bot, and 10 team members.
- Website chat, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, Discord and Email.
- Shopify, WooCommerce and WordPress.
- A unified inbox, knowledge base, custom tools and human handoff with full context.
- Conversation analytics, email support, and a limited REST API.

## What does the Pro plan include?
Pro costs $79 a month ($63 a month billed yearly). It includes everything in Starter, plus:
- About 50,000 estimated AI replies and 50M AI tokens per month.
- 10 bots, 1,000 knowledge documents per bot, and 25 team members.
- The full REST API and webhooks.
- Web search for the AI agent, as listed on the pricing page.
- Priority support.
- HubSpot, Zendesk, Slack and Microsoft Teams, which are coming soon (see the channels article).

## What does the Enterprise plan include?
Enterprise is priced on request. It includes everything in Pro, plus:
- Unlimited AI replies and tokens.
- Up to 100 bots, 1,000 knowledge documents per bot, and 500 team members, with custom limits available.
- Hands-on onboarding for your channels and tools.
- Dedicated support.
To get a quote, use https://truplexy.com/demo?topic=sales.

## What are AI tokens?
Tokens are how AI models measure text: short pieces of words. Every reply uses tokens for what the assistant reads (the customer's message, the conversation so far and the knowledge it looks up) and for what it writes. The monthly token allowance resets each month.

## What does "est. AI replies" mean?
Est. AI replies is a guide to how many replies your monthly tokens cover at a typical length. Long conversations and long knowledge passages use more tokens per reply, so your real number can be higher or lower.

## Can I buy extra tokens?
Yes. Extra tokens cost $8 per 1M on Starter, with up to 5M extra per month. On Pro they cost $6 per 1M, with up to 30M extra per month. Enterprise add-ons are custom. Add-on tokens expire at the end of the month they were bought in and do not roll over.

## What happens when my monthly tokens run out?
Further replies and add-on tokens are paid from your account balance. If the balance reaches zero, the assistant stops replying until you add funds or the next month starts.

## Who processes payments?
Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited) is the Merchant of Record. It takes payment, collects and pays any sales tax or VAT, and issues invoices.

## Can I change plans?
Yes. You can move to a different plan at any time. A move to a cheaper plan, or to a shorter billing period, takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for.

## Do prices change?
Truplexy gives at least 30 days' notice before a price change takes effect for your plan. If you don't agree, cancel before it takes effect.

## Is there a free plan?
No. The Free plan was retired. New accounts start with a 7-day free trial of Starter or Pro. See the free trial article.
