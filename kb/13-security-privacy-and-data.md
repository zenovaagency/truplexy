# Truplexy — Security, Privacy and Data

Full text: https://truplexy.com/privacy and https://truplexy.com/terms

## What does Truplexy do with my data and my customers' data?
You decide what goes into Truplexy, and Truplexy processes your customers' conversations on your behalf. To write a reply, only the relevant part of the conversation and the knowledge passages needed are sent to the AI model provider. Zenova Agency does not train AI models on your content or your customers' conversations, and does not sell personal data.

## Is the connection encrypted?
Yes. Connections use HTTPS, and platform access tokens are stored encrypted.

## Which companies process data for Truplexy?
- Vercel: hosting for the website, dashboard and API.
- Supabase: sign-in and real-time updates.
- Cloudflare: file storage, database, search index and embeddings.
- OpenRouter and the model providers behind it: write the assistant's replies.
- Tavily: optional web search, only where an assistant is set up to use it.
- Paddle: payments and Merchant of Record.
- jsDelivr: delivers the chat widget script when you use the CDN snippet.
- Meta: advertising measurement on truplexy.com, only if you accept the cookie banner.

## Do the AI model providers keep my data?
Which model is used depends on the business's settings, so the provider can differ. Each provider's own terms govern how long it keeps requests. Truplexy does not train models on your content.

## How long is conversation history kept?
Conversation history is kept for the period of the plan: 90 days on Starter, 1 year on Pro, and 7 days while a business has no plan.

## What happens when I delete my business?
A deleted business can be restored for 30 days. After that, all of its records, knowledge-base files and search vectors are permanently erased. Billing and tax records are kept as long as the law requires.

## Does the chat widget set cookies?
No. It keeps a signed session token and the visitor's details in the browser's local storage. The Truplexy website has a cookie banner only for optional advertising measurement.

## Can I be sure AI answers are always correct?
No. AI replies can be wrong. The assistant answers from your knowledge and says when it can't find a reliable answer, and your team can read and correct any conversation.

## Where do I send a privacy request?
Email hello@truplexy.com. You can ask for your data to be corrected, deleted or exported. Knowledge-base files, customers and tickets can also be deleted in the dashboard.

## Is Truplexy for children?
No. Truplexy is for businesses, and account holders must be at least 18.
