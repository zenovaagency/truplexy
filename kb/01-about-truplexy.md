# Truplexy — About

## What is Truplexy?
Truplexy is an independent AI customer support platform. It puts an AI assistant on every channel a business's customers use, and gives the business's team one platform to manage every conversation. The tagline is "Every channel. One platform."

## What does Truplexy do?
Truplexy answers customers for a business on every channel it uses, in the team's tone, day and night. It brings every conversation into one inbox for the team. When a person adds value, the team takes over with the conversation and context already there.

## Who is Truplexy for?
Truplexy is for businesses that support customers on more than one channel. Small teams use the Starter plan. Larger organisations that need custom limits use Enterprise. Truplexy is for businesses, so account holders must be at least 18.

## What problems does Truplexy solve?
Truplexy solves four problems:
- Teams jumping between separate apps for each channel.
- Messages that get missed.
- Questions that arrive outside business hours.
- Chatbots that leave people stuck with no way to reach a person.

## How is Truplexy different from a traditional chatbot?
A typical chatbot lives on one channel and loops through canned answers. Truplexy runs on many channels, answers from the business's own knowledge with the source attached, puts every conversation in one inbox, and hands over to a person instead of dead-ending.
